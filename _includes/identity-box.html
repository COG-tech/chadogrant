About Chad O. Grant

Chad O. Grant is an author, macro commentator, creator of S.C.O.P.E. Report, founder of Marathon Macro, and writer of Dawn Archives. His work connects global economics, geopolitics, history, investing, national strength, and long-form storytelling.

This GitHub Pages site is the official online hub for Chad O. Grant's books, essays, videos, macro research, fiction, and public profiles.
