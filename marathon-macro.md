  <h2>Global macro research</h2>
  <p>
    Marathon Macro is the research and commentary lane tied to Chad O.
    Grant's broader macro work. It focuses on the long arc of economics,
    geopolitics, markets, and national strength rather than short-term noise.
  </p>
  <p>
    The idea is simple: follow the structural forces that shape the next cycle
    before they become obvious. That makes the page a useful anchor for
    long-horizon thinking about investing, policy, trade, and sovereign
    pressure.
